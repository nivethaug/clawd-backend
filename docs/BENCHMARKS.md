## Reading
- Eager 3D imports multiply the initial bundle by **1.0×** — which is why the 3d preset ships `src/lib/lazy3d.tsx` and the agent prompt forbids eager three/fiber/drei imports in shared components.
- The `manualChunks` vendor rule (added with the 3d preset) is what keeps three out of the entry chunk in EVERY wiring — even eager imports land at 310 KB initial (they were 1180 KB before the rule, live on project 2131). Without it, two dynamic entries sharing three made Rollup hoist a duplicate into the entry chunk.
